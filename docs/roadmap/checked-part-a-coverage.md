### Scalar-base extension disposition

The two [archived manifest entries](https://github.com/TechSpokes/typescript-wsdl-client/blob/460f5b8379c68ffef79917284e445b5ab046429e/test/conformance/schema-assessment-manifest.json) identify the exact inputs. Both define complex base `B` with simple string content and derive `D` using complex-content extension. The archives agree on libxml2 acceptance and disagree on the empty sequence in xmlschema. The independent disposition follows [XSD 1.0 Structures §3.4.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2004/REC-xmlschema-1-20041028/#Complex_Type_Definition_details) and [§3.4.6](https://www.w3.org/TR/2004/REC-xmlschema-1-20041028/#cos-ct-extends).

Absent local content and an empty local sequence each map to empty effective content when effective mixed is false. Clause 3.2.1 inherits the same scalar component. Extension has to satisfy the base's final constraint, preserve attribute uses and wildcard obligations, and establish the in-principle extension and restriction witness. A nonempty particle or effective mixed content cannot satisfy the scalar-content branch.

On v1.8.1 generated legacy clients for both forms emitted the expected request text and decoded `{$value: "wire output"}` from an owned endpoint. Checked native construction returned `unresolved/effective-complex-content` and planning reported `whole-source-qualification:native-construction-unestablished`. The ordinary `simpleContent` control qualified. The legacy control over a complex simple-content base wrapped its response text twice, while checked returns the single source-owned `$value`.

The [maintained regression](../../test/integration/checked-scalar-base-extension.test.ts) pins each original archive digest, verifies the native witness and checks the generated pipeline. Invalid input dispatches no SOAP, an invalid upstream particle yields no typed success, stale source refuses before dispatch, and adjacent mixed, nonempty and final controls stay refused.

## Continuing scope

The legacy QName projection exposed spelling and left namespace meaning to the caller. At v1.8.1 checked generation refused the direct QName case with `scalar-correspondence-scope`. The replacement follows [XSD 1.0 Datatypes §3.2.18](https://www.w3.org/TR/2004/REC-xmlschema-2-20041028/#QName) and preserves scoped lexical contexts for unions. Prefix-dependent pattern witnesses and document-wide identifiers remain with #179/#184/#187.

Part B owns standalone generation and app workflow parity, multi-source composition and streaming. Wider RPC, attachments, policies and encoding investigation retain #427/#428/#429/#462. Default activation and legacy retirement are separately governed by #164/#204/#205/#208.
